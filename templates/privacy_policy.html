Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PrivateConnect. PrivateConnect is a social communication application that allows users to create profiles, connect with other users, share posts and communicate through private messages.

2. Information We Collect

PrivateConnect may collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Password in securely hashed form
  • Profile picture
  • Bio and profile information
  • Posts and captions
  • Images and other uploaded media
  • Messages and images sent through chat
  • Connections and friend information
  • Likes and comments

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Display your profile
  • Allow users to connect with each other
  • Provide messaging features
  • Allow image and media sharing
  • Display posts, likes and comments
  • Maintain application security
  • Improve PrivateConnect

4. Messages and Private Communication

PrivateConnect provides private messaging between users. Messages and media may be stored so they can be delivered and displayed to participants in the conversation.

Users should avoid sharing passwords, financial information or other highly sensitive information through messages.

5. Profile and User Content

Information and content that you choose to share through your profile or posts may be visible to other PrivateConnect users.

6. Password Security

PrivateConnect does not intentionally store passwords as plain text. Passwords are processed using password hashing mechanisms.

7. Sessions

PrivateConnect may use session technologies to keep users logged in and maintain secure communication with the application.

8. Sharing of Information

PrivateConnect does not sell your personal information. Information may be disclosed when necessary to operate the application, protect users, investigate security issues or comply with applicable legal requirements.

9. Data Retention

Account information and user-generated content may be retained as necessary to provide the application's services or for legitimate security and legal purposes.

10. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their PrivateConnect account and associated personal information through the account deletion options provided by the application.

11. Children's Privacy

PrivateConnect is not intended for children below the minimum age permitted by applicable law.

12. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by PrivateConnect. However, no internet service can guarantee complete security.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PrivateConnect's features or data practices change. The latest version will be made available through the application.

14. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request account deletion, please contact the PrivateConnect administrator through the contact information provided by the application.
